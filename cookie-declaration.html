Change or withdraw your choices

Use Cookie settings on any page. Optional categories start off, and accepting is not required to browse. Your choice is remembered for up to six months; a change to our purposes may require a new choice sooner. Rejecting is remembered for the same period as accepting.

Withdrawing a previously allowed category reloads the page to stop the service running in that page. We also remove recognised, accessible first-party analytics and marketing cookies. We cannot remove third-party cookies or data already collected by another provider; use your browser controls and the provider’s privacy tools for those.

Other connections and your information

The site also uses Cloudflare for hosting and security, Google Fonts for typefaces, and external links such as Calendly and social media. Ordinary external links connect when you follow them. These are distinct from optional tracking choices; hosting/security may process technical request data to deliver and protect the site. Google Fonts stylesheet and font requests connect to Google when pages load.

Submitting an enquiry or newsletter form sends the information you enter to the relevant service. Loading a form or accepting cookies is not itself consent to receive marketing emails. Our cookie-choice record stays in your browser; this site does not keep a separate server-side consent audit log.

This is a maintained service inventory, not an automated Cookiebot scan. Additional tags or embeds must be reviewed and added to the consent controls before they are published.
